About me

I've always admired people who live diverse, storied lives — who have ripped apart the rulebook and followed a path of their own making. My career journey has routed me through various fields, including science, local and national politics, international relations and geopolitics, community building, journalism, live TV news, tech and AI, and now freelance video production. Throughout it all my mission never changed: to inform the public on what matters.

Most traditional corporations would find fault in my nonlinear background. If you're someone who sees the value in learning every day, adjusting to new challenges, thinking on your feet, never settling for mediocrity, and above all, making cool and impactful content, then I'd be thrilled to work together.

Tina Huang

  • From scratchInstagram presence rebuilt
  • 2 platformsInstagram + YouTube
  • Idea → publishEnd-to-end short-form production

The goal

Build Tina's Instagram presence from scratch while strengthening her YouTube content strategy and production.

What I did

Tina was locked out of her Instagram account and hadn't posted in months. I developed a new short-form content strategy, which included coming up with content ideas, scripting, editing, publishing, and ongoing optimization. I also supported her YouTube channel through video ideation, outlining, production management, and content strategy.

The impact

Created a consistent, scalable content system designed to grow Tina's audience across platforms while allowing her to focus on creating.
